Timber shapes the feeling of a home more than any other material. The three below account for most of our projects.

## Walnut

Deep chocolate brown with a soft, swirling grain. Walnut makes a space feel calm and refined at once, but can weigh down a small room. Use it with restraint: one wall, a dining table, the doors.

## Oak

Pale yellow to honey brown with a clear straight grain. Oak is durable, easy to combine and takes stain well — from ash white to smoked. It is the safest choice for floors and joinery.

## Ash

Lighter than oak, with a bold, high-contrast grain. Ash suits younger, brighter apartments and is kinder on the budget.

## Solid timber or veneer?

For cabinet doors and large panels, veneer on a stable core usually performs better than solid wood: less warping in a humid climate and more even in colour. Keep solid timber for table tops, handrails and the places hands touch every day.

Whichever you choose, ask for a real sample and look at it in the light of your own home, both morning and evening.
